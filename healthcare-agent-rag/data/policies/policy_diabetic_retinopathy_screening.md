# Policy PA-211: Diabetic Retinopathy Screening Coverage

**Document ID:** PA-211 · **Effective:** synthetic, for demonstration only · **Category:** Ophthalmology — Preventive Screening

## Section 1: Coverage Criteria

Annual diabetic retinopathy screening (dilated fundus exam or validated retinal imaging, e.g. fundus photography) is covered WITHOUT prior authorization for members with:

1. Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes mellitus diagnosis (ICD-10 E10.x or E11.x series) on file.
2. No screening claim on record within the preceding 11 months (screening frequency limit: once per 12-month rolling window).

## Section 2: Frequency Limits and Exceptions

- Standard frequency: once every 12 months for members with no retinopathy findings on the prior exam.
- Members with documented mild non-proliferative diabetic retinopathy on a prior exam are eligible for screening every 6 months without additional prior authorization.
- Members with moderate or severe non-proliferative or any proliferative diabetic retinopathy findings should be referred for treatment evaluation (see Policy PA-233, glaucoma and retinal treatment authorization) rather than routine screening.

## Section 3: Required Documentation

- No prior authorization required for routine annual screening within frequency limits.
- For the 6-month accelerated screening frequency, the prior exam's findings (mild NPDR) must be on file to justify the shortened interval; if not on file, claim will be held for records request rather than denied outright.

## Section 4: Common Denial Reasons

- **Frequency limit exceeded** — screening claim submitted less than 11 months after the prior screening claim, with no accelerated-frequency justification (mild NPDR finding) on file.
- **Diagnosis code not on file** — diabetes diagnosis not present in claims history; resubmit with diagnosis-confirming documentation from the referring provider.
