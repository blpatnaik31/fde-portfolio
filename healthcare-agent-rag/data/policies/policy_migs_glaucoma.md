# Policy PA-233: Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS) Prior Authorization

**Document ID:** PA-233 · **Effective:** synthetic, for demonstration only · **Category:** Ophthalmology — Glaucoma Treatment

## Section 1: Coverage Criteria

MIGS procedures are covered under prior authorization when ALL of the following are met:

1. Confirmed diagnosis of mild to moderate primary open-angle glaucoma (ICD-10 H40.11x series) with documented intraocular pressure (IOP) above target despite maximum tolerated medical therapy (minimum 2 topical agents, documented trial of at least 90 days).
2. Documented visual field testing (Humphrey visual field or equivalent) within the prior 6 months showing glaucomatous field loss consistent with diagnosis.
3. Optical coherence tomography (OCT) of the optic nerve head on file within the prior 6 months.
4. MIGS procedure is being performed in combination with, or subsequent to, cataract surgery (standalone MIGS without concurrent or prior cataract surgery requires additional medical director review).

## Section 2: Required Documentation for Prior Auth Submission

- IOP measurements over the medical-therapy trial period (minimum 2 readings, 90+ days apart)
- Documented medication list showing 2+ topical agents attempted
- Visual field test report (within 6 months)
- OCT report (within 6 months)

## Section 3: Common Denial Reasons

- **Insufficient medical therapy trial** — fewer than 2 topical agents documented, or trial period under 90 days. Resubmit once trial criteria are met, or request exception review for documented medication intolerance.
- **Missing or outdated visual field / OCT testing** — most common documentation gap; resubmit with current-window testing attached.
- **Standalone MIGS without cataract surgery linkage** — requires medical director review rather than standard prior-auth pathway; route to exception queue.
