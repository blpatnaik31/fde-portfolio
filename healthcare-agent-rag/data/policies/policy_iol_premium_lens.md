# Policy PA-104: Premium Intraocular Lens (IOL) Prior Authorization

**Document ID:** PA-104 · **Effective:** synthetic, for demonstration only · **Category:** Ophthalmology — Cataract Surgery

## Section 1: Coverage Criteria

Premium intraocular lenses (toric, multifocal, and extended depth-of-focus IOLs) are covered under prior authorization when ALL of the following are met:

1. Member has a confirmed diagnosis of visually significant cataract (ICD-10 H25.x series) with documented best-corrected visual acuity of 20/40 or worse in the affected eye.
2. Preoperative corneal topography or biometry (IOLMaster or equivalent partial coherence interferometry) has been performed within 90 days of the planned surgery date.
3. For toric IOLs specifically: documented corneal astigmatism of 1.0 diopter or greater, confirmed by two independent measurement methods.
4. Member has been counseled on the difference between standard monofocal IOL (covered without additional authorization) and premium IOL options, with signed acknowledgment of any applicable out-of-pocket refractive-upgrade cost on file.

## Section 2: Required Documentation for Prior Auth Submission

- Biometry report (axial length, keratometry, anterior chamber depth)
- Corneal topography report (for toric lens requests)
- Visual acuity testing results, both eyes, best-corrected
- Signed refractive-upgrade acknowledgment form

## Section 3: CPT and Modifier Requirements

- Base cataract extraction with IOL insertion: CPT 66984
- Premium lens refractive upgrade is billed as a non-covered patient-responsibility line item alongside CPT 66984; the premium lens itself does not carry a separate CPT code under this policy.
- Bilateral procedures performed in separate encounters require modifier -RT or -LT on each claim; same-day bilateral procedures are not authorized under this policy except with documented medical necessity for same-day bilateral surgery (rare, requires separate medical director review).

## Section 4: Common Denial Reasons

- **Missing biometry within 90-day window** — resubmit with updated biometry report.
- **Astigmatism measurement below 1.0 diopter for toric request** — standard monofocal IOL is the covered alternative; toric upgrade is member's out-of-pocket responsibility, not a denial requiring resubmission.
- **Missing signed refractive-upgrade acknowledgment** — most common documentation gap; resubmit with signed form attached.
